# Executive Fraud Operations Dashboard Guide

## 1. Executive KPIs

- **Alerts Processed Today**: Total alert volume detected and prioritized.
- **Critical Alerts**: P0/P1 count requiring priority oversight.
- **Confirmed Fraud Value**: Actual exposure associated with validated fraud alerts.
- **SLA Compliance Rate**: Overall percentage of alerts resolved within target SLA windows.
- **Active Fraud Networks**: Count of active syndicates and collusive rings under monitoring.

---

## 2. Detector Operational Confirmation Analytics

Distinguishes **operational confirmation rate** (investigator consensus) from theoretical ML precision/recall:

$$\text{Operational Confirmation Rate} = \frac{\text{Confirmed Fraud Alerts}}{\text{Confirmed Fraud Alerts} + \text{False Positive Alerts}} \times 100\%$$

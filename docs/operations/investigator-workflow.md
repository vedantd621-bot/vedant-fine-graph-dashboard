# Investigator Operations Workflow Guide

## 1. Workstation Structure

The Investigator Operations Workstation (`/investigator-hub`) consolidates operational tools:

1. **My Queue**: Personal assigned alerts with real-time countdown timers and 1-click triage buttons.
2. **Critical Alerts Stream (P0)**: P0/P1 emergency alerts requiring immediate forensic assessment.
3. **Active Cases**: Open investigation dossiers with direct linking to evidence items and graph subgraphs.
4. **Team Workload Overview**: Real-time capacity, open cases, critical loads, and operational confirmation rates.

---

## 2. Investigation Actions & Shortcuts

- **Open Subgraph**: Launches interactive D3 force-directed view of the suspect cluster.
- **Inspect Money Trail**: Calculates flow hops and velocity bottlenecks across intermediary mules.
- **Explain Priority**: Inspects exact mathematical factor breakdowns and evidence summaries.
- **Promote to Case**: Upgrades network or alert clusters into formal investigation cases with cryptographic evidence items.
